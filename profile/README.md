### THE INFORMATION EXISTS.
### **THE CONTEXT IS FRAGMENTED.**

</div>

Modern freight operations span a complex network of systems — freight platforms, carrier and airline systems, customs portals, spreadsheets, email, documents, and messaging.

Each system contains part of the operational picture.

Teams are still left to connect those pieces manually to understand **what is happening, what matters, and what needs to happen next.**

---

## What We're Building

**Frintl is building the operational intelligence layer for freight-forwarding teams.**

We connect fragmented operational signals and build a unified understanding of what is happening across shipments and workflows.

Rather than replacing the systems teams already rely on, Frintl is designed to work **alongside existing infrastructure** — turning the information already generated by an operation into context that teams can understand and act upon.

## Why Frintl

Freight teams don't need another place to recreate the operation.

They need a clearer understanding of the operation that already exists.

Frintl is being built to help teams:

- Understand what is happening across fragmented workflows
- Identify exceptions before they become larger operational problems
- Surface missing, inconsistent, or time-sensitive information
- Understand the impact of operational changes
- Prioritize what requires attention
- Determine what needs to happen next

<br>

<div align="center">

> **The goal isn't more information.**
>
> **The goal is better operational understanding.**

</div>

## Built Alongside the Industry

We are starting with **freight-forwarding operations** and building alongside industry teams around real workflows, existing infrastructure, and measurable operational problems.
